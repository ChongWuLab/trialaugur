Submit your CSV through the submission form. An automatic check validates the file within a few minutes, records the submission time and stores accepted forecasts in the public repository.

Open the submission form Download the NCT IDs (CSV)

The form is a GitHub issue form, so a free GitHub account is needed. Attach the CSV by dragging it into the form; the reply on the issue says whether it was accepted and, if not, what to fix.

Rules

  • Record before readout. Only forecasts submitted while a trial is still pending are scored for that trial. The submission time is the time the submission form (a GitHub issue) was opened, or last edited, as recorded by GitHub.
  • No edits after readout. You may update forecasts for pending trials; a trial's forecast is frozen at its last value before readout.
  • Any evidence dated before readout is allowed. Literature, registries, filings, web search: anything, as long as it predates the readout.
  • Coverage is flexible. Forecast any subset of pending trials; metrics are reported with the number of trials covered.

How outcomes are labelled

Trials are classified as a success or a failure according to whether the reported results satisfy the prespecified criteria for the primary endpoints. Explicit statements of primary endpoint attainment or failure in the readout are used when consistent with the trial's prespecified design. When such statements are absent or ambiguous, the reported results are compared with the primary endpoints documented in ClinicalTrials.gov. Trials terminated before completion are failures, unless attainment of the prespecified primary endpoint has been reported.

For trials with multiple primary endpoints, adjudication follows the prespecified criteria for overall trial success: trials requiring all coprimary endpoints to be met are failures if any required endpoint failed, and trials permitting success on a subset of primary endpoints are evaluated according to their prespecified multiplicity adjusted testing strategy. A trial stays unresolved, and is not scored, when the available results or prespecified decision criteria are insufficient to determine whether the primary endpoints were met, or when conflicting information cannot be resolved. Every label links its primary source, and the same rules apply regardless of how a sponsor frames the disclosure.
