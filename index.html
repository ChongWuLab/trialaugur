How it works

01

Curate

TrialDossier, evidence agent

In stage 1, TrialDossier uses the NCT ID to retrieve and extract evidence from ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, Open Targets, openFDA, prespecified web sources and company disclosures, organizing it into 28 fields. Stage 2 standardizes or condenses eight fields and retains the remaining 20 without semantic rewriting. Information that cannot be retrieved is explicitly recorded as missing.

02

Forecast

TrialOutlook, prediction system

TrialOutlook supplies the dossier to a GPT-5 reasoning expert, prompted to estimate the probability that the trial meets its prespecified primary endpoint and to update it with the dossier evidence. A probability of at least 0.5 is a prediction of success.

03

Adjudicate

Prespecified outcome criteria

Success requires evidence that the prespecified primary endpoint was met, from an explicit statement in the outcome disclosure or, when none is available, by comparing the reported results with the primary endpoint registered on ClinicalTrials.gov. Failure requires evidence of a missed primary endpoint or trial discontinuation without reported attainment of the primary endpoint. Otherwise the outcome remains unresolved.
